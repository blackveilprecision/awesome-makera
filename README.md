## Official Resources

- [Makera GitHub](https://github.com/MakeraInc) - Official organization hosting the controller, firmware, MakeraCAM releases and machine profiles.
- [Makera Support](https://www.makera.com/pages/support) - Official support hub for technical, software and order help, with links to the wiki.
- [Makera Wiki](https://wiki.makera.com/) - Official manuals, troubleshooting, maintenance guides and project sharing for Makera machines.
- [Manual & Examples Guide](https://www.makera.com/pages/carvera-manual-examples) - Downloads of the official instruction manuals and example project guides.

## Machines

- [Carvera](https://www.makera.com/products/carvera) - Original enclosed desktop CNC mill (C1) with automatic tool changer, auto probing, vacuum system and laser engraver.
- [Carvera Air](https://www.makera.com/products/carvera-air) - Compact enclosed desktop CNC (CA1) with quick manual tool change, 300x200x130 mm travel and optional 4th axis.
- [Carvera vs Carvera Air](https://www.makera.com/blogs/cnc-buying-guide/carvera-carvera-air-which-desktop-cnc-is-right-for-you) - Official comparison to help choose between the two Carvera models.
- [Makera Z1](https://www.makera.com/products/makera-z1-desktop-cnc) - Entry-level die-cast desktop CNC with quick tool changer, camera and auto probing, sold in Z1 and Z1 Pro variants.
- [Makera Z1 vs Z1 Pro](https://www.makera.com/blogs/cnc-buying-guide/makera-z1-vs-z1-pro) - Official comparison of the Z1 and the Z1 Pro, which adds closed-loop steppers and ball screws.

## Controllers & Apps

- [Carve Control](https://github.com/GridSpace/carve-control) - Open-source web-based controller and network proxy for remote access to Carvera machines.
- [Carvera Controller](https://github.com/MakeraInc/CarveraController) - Official open-source controller for Carvera and Carvera Air on desktop and mobile.
- [Carvera Controller Community](https://github.com/Carvera-Community/Carvera_Controller) - Open-source community fork of the controller with advanced probing, pendant support and many fixes.
- [Makera App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/makera-cnc-maker-hub/id6758646579) - Official mobile companion app for machine access and browsing Makerables projects, also on Android.
- [OpenSpindle](https://github.com/openspindle/openspindle) - Open-source desktop app for setting up, simulating and running Z1 jobs, with probing, Fusion 360 import and PCB prep; early alpha.

## Firmware

- [Carvera Community Firmware](https://github.com/Carvera-Community/Carvera_Community_Firmware) - Open-source community Smoothieware branch adding 3D probe support, variables, Air ATC support and more.
- [Carvera Firmware](https://github.com/MakeraInc/CarveraFirmware) - Official open-source Smoothieware-based firmware for both Carvera and Carvera Air.
- [Makera Z1 Firmware](https://github.com/MakeraInc/MakeraZ1Firmware) - Official open-source Smoothieware-based motion-control firmware for the Z1 and Z1 Pro.

## CAM & Post-Processors

- [Carvera Community Fusion Plugin](https://github.com/Carvera-Community/Carvera_Community_FusionPlugin) - Beta Fusion add-in that splits post output per operation, setup or tool and supports A-axis indexing.
- [Carvera Community Profiles](https://github.com/Carvera-Community/Carvera_Community_Profiles) - Community-maintained Fusion, VCarve, FreeCAD and LightBurn posts, machine files and tool libraries.
- [Carvera Profiles](https://github.com/MakeraInc/CarveraProfiles) - Official Fusion machine files, tool libraries and posts, plus VCarve posts and a LightBurn device profile.
- [Community HSM Tool Library](https://github.com/Carvera-Community/Carvera_Community_Profiles/releases/tag/toollibraries) - Makera shop cutters in HSMLib format for import into Fusion, SolidWorks and HSMAdvisor.
- [EasyCAM5000](https://github.com/RicardoJCMarques/EasyCAM5000) - Open-source browser-based CAM for PCB and 2.5D/3D milling with a community-tested Makera post.
- [Kiri:Moto](https://grid.space/kiri/) - Open-source browser-based CAM and slicer that includes Carvera and Carvera Air device profiles.
- [MakeraCAM](https://www.makera.com/pages/makera-cam) - Official free CAM for the Carvera series, from 2D and PCB milling to 3D carving.
- [Makera Studio](https://www.makera.com/pages/software) - Official free CAM, simulation and machine-control suite for Carvera, Carvera Air and Z1, succeeding MakeraCAM.

## Macros & Utilities

- [BVP Z1-Macros](https://github.com/blackveilprecision/z1-macros) - Open-source G-code utilities for leveling stock, tool changes, and other Z1 workflow tasks.

## Accessories

- [Carvera 4th Axis Module](https://www.makera.com/products/carvera-4th-axis-module) - Official harmonic-drive rotary axis for the original Carvera with homing and work-coordinate support.
- [Carvera Air Add-ons](https://www.makera.com/collections/add-ons-carvera-air) - Official add-ons for the Carvera Air, including the 4th axis and 5 W laser modules.
- [Makera 3D Wired Probe](https://www.makera.com/products/makera-3d-wired-probe) - Official 3-axis touch probe for the Z1 and Carvera Air; not compatible with the original Carvera.
- [Makera Z1 Add-ons](https://www.makera.com/collections/add-ons-z1) - Official Z1 add-ons, including the 4th axis module, laser module and cyclone dust collector.

## Upgrades & Printable Mods

- [Carvera Air ATC Mod](https://www.instructables.com/Carvera-Air-ATC-Mod/) - Work-in-progress build guide that adds an automatic tool changer to the Carvera Air using community firmware.
- [Carvera Back Panel Isolation](https://www.printables.com/model/873426-carvera-back-panel-isolation-3d-prints) - Printed parts that seal off the back of the original Carvera from chips.
- [Carvera Tool Caddy Mk3](https://www.printables.com/model/700389-carvera-makera-tool-caddy-mk3) - Printable caddy holding commonly used Carvera tools and clamping hardware.
- [Carvera Touch Probe Modifications](https://www.instructables.com/Carvera-Touch-Probe-Modifications/) - Wiring guide for fitting a 3D touch probe to the Carvera Air and the original Carvera.
- [Makera Z1 Bed CAD Files](https://github.com/cinetronixlabs/makera-z1-bed-cad-files) - Unofficial STEP and DXF references of the Z1 and Z1 Pro bed for designing fixtures.
- [Open-Source 3-Axis CNC Touch Probe](https://github.com/faecorrigan/Open-Source-3-axis-CNC-Touch-Probe) - Wireless probe with OTA updates and wireless charging, developed primarily for the Carvera.
- [Printables Carvera Models](https://www.printables.com/search/models?q=tag:carvera) - Community printable mods, holders and fixtures tagged for Carvera machines.

## Tooling & Workholding

- [Bits Collar](https://www.makera.com/products/bits-collar) - Official collars in 3, 4, 6 and 6.35 mm sizes that let bits work with the Carvera automatic tool changer.
- [CNC Bits](https://www.makera.com/collections/cnc-bits) - Official Makera end mills, ball nose, V-bits, drills and thread mills sized for Makera machines.
- [Makers Carvera Waste Board](https://www.printables.com/model/942053-makers-carvera-waste-board) - Printable waste board that lets standard bolts clamp stock on the Carvera.
- [Saunders Makera Fixture Plates](https://saundersmachineworks.com/collections/makera-fixture-plates) - Commercial 6061 aluminum fixture plates for Carvera, Carvera Air and Z1.
- [Workholding Techniques for Small CNC Machines](https://www.makera.com/blogs/article/best-workholding-techniques-for-small-cnc-machines) - Official guide to vises, clamps, fixture plates and vacuum setups for Carvera machines.

## Guides & Tutorials

- [Anatomy of a Carvera](https://carvera-community.gitbook.io/docs/anatomy-of-a-carvera) - Community breakdown of the specifications and internal components of the Carvera family.
- [Carvera Air Manual](https://wiki.makera.com/en/Air/Manual) - Official digital version of the Carvera Air instruction manual and examples guide.
- [Carvera How-To Wiki](https://wiki.makera.com/en/carvera/How-To) - Official wiki tutorials and guides for operating the original Carvera.
- [Carvera PCB Guide](https://github.com/brunostjohn/CarveraPCBGuide) - Double-sided PCB workflow on the Carvera Air with KiCad, FlatCAM, MakeraCAM, UV solder mask and laser silkscreen.
- [Carvera PCB Tutorial](https://quentinbolsee.pages.cba.mit.edu/carvera-pcb-tutorial/) - MIT CBA tutorial on milling PCBs with the Carvera, using tool changes for traces and board outlines.
- [CNC Machining A to Z](https://github.com/Carvera-Community/cnc_a_to_z) - Community adaptation of the Shapeoko CNC A to Z beginner guidebook with Carvera-specific content.
- [Community Software Documentation](https://carvera-community.gitbook.io/docs) - Docs for the community controller, firmware, Fusion post and plugin, plus guides and mods.
- [Getting Started with CNC Machines](https://wiki.makera.com/en/GettingStarted) - Official wiki primer preparing beginners for subtractive manufacturing on Makera machines.
- [Speeds & Feeds for CNC Projects](https://www.instructables.com/How-to-Set-the-Speeds-Feeds-for-Your-CNC-Projects/) - Makera's step-by-step guide to choosing spindle speeds and feed rates.
