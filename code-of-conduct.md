# Code of Conduct

This list is a shared, friendly space for people who make things with Makera machines, from first-time desktop CNC owners to small production shops.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with newcomers and people whose first language isn't English.
- Give constructive feedback about resources, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and disclose your affiliation when you suggest something you make or sell.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Spam, undisclosed advertising, or repeatedly submitting the same resource after it was declined.
- Publishing other people's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block accounts that don't follow this code. To report a problem privately, contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile.
